WASD / Arrows move  ·  E grab · chop (hold) · grill · stack · serve  ·  Click a station to walk over  ·  P pause  ·  M mute  ·  R restart

Sizzle Station is an original cooking game: chop the veg, grill the patty, stack the plate to match the order board, and ring the bell before patience runs out. Reach 150 coins before the 3:00 shift ends — the last minute is rush hour. No network, no files beyond this folder; all art is drawn in code and all sounds are synthesized.
